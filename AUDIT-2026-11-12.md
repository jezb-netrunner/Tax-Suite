# JEZ Tax Suite: Audit of November 12, 2026

Branch `claude/hopeful-mccarthy-or1089`, from `main` at d14bb4c. Audit dates: **November 11-12, 2026** (Asia/Manila).
This follows the review of October 7, 2026 (REVIEW.md, 78 findings) and the audit of November 8, 2026
(AUDIT-2026-11.md, 54 findings). It covers five areas: the law in force today, the deadline calendar, the
calculators, data safety and internal control, and the screens. Everything found was fixed unless section 5 says
otherwise.

Nothing was deployed. Nothing was pushed to `main`. No live database was touched.

## 1. Summary for the owner

**Status: ready to publish once you fill in two contact details (section 7).** The rate tables and the arithmetic
were re-checked by hand and in a browser and are right: the graduated table, 8%, OSD, percentage tax, the four
withholding tables, SSS / PhilHealth / Pag-IBIG, corporate RCIT / MCIT and the penalty calculator all gave the
hand-computed answer. What was wrong was around them: three deadline changes issued in the last three weeks, two
corporate rules, one misleading Estimator screen, and several ways a user could lose saved data.

61 findings: **2 Critical, 12 High, 23 Medium, 24 Low.** The most important, in order:

1. **Three BIR deadline changes were not in the app (Critical / High).**
   - **RMC 118-2026**: returns due **November 10, 2026** filed through eBIRForms may be filed until **November 13,
     2026** (the eBIRForms Offline Package was down). The app showed the October 1601-C, 0619-E and 0619-F as already
     past due. Fixed.
   - **BIR tax advisory (reported Nov 11)**: taxpayers under the listed **Metro Manila** revenue district offices may
     file returns due **November 16-18, 2026** until **November 19, 2026** (ASEAN Summit holidays). This answers
     open question 1 of the last audit. The calendar keeps Nov 16 (always safe) and now states the Nov 19 date.
   - **RMC 115-2026**: the Q3 2026 Forms 2550Q and 2551Q moved from October 26 to **October 29, 2026** (eFPS outage).
2. **MCIT for fiscal-year corporations was shown "both ways" when the rule is clear (High).** RR 9-98 counts from the
   calendar year of registration: a firm registered on July 1, 1998 pays MCIT for the whole fiscal year ending in
   2002. So a corporation registered any month of 2022 is under MCIT for its fiscal year ending in 2026. Fixed.
3. **NOLCO was left out of the quarterly corporate return (High).** RR 14-2001 allows NOLCO on the quarterly and the
   annual returns. The 1702Q view now deducts it (never against the MCIT).
4. **The Estimator showed the amount payable for the cheapest option, not the taxpayer's own (High).** A freelancer
   locked into graduated + OSD saw the 8% breakdown and the 8% "payable with the annual return". The breakdown and
   the return preview now follow the regime on the profile; any other option can be opened.
5. **Saved data could be lost in four ways (High).** There was a "Download my data" but no way to put a file back;
   unreadable saved data was shown as "No profiles yet" and the next save erased it; browsers may clear a site's
   storage on their own; and an old copy of the app never said its rules were old. All four are addressed
   (section 3.3).
6. **Amount boxes could show a number the app was not using (High).** Typing `480000,50` and leaving the box turned
   it into `48,000,050` while the estimate went on using ₱480,000. Fixed.
7. **Calendar gaps (High).** The monthly **eSales report** for cash register / POS users was missing; mandatory
   e-invoicing was shown to POS users who are not yet covered; corporations were not told about the SEC's **HARBOR**
   beneficial-ownership registry; Pag-IBIG's move to eSRS was described from the old circular.

### Limits of the legal check (please read)

The BIR, SEC, SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, Official Gazette and law-library websites cannot be opened from this
environment (the network policy blocks them). The law was checked by web search: issuance texts as they appear in
search results, firm alerts (Grant Thornton, KPMG, PwC, Deloitte) and news reports (Inquirer, Tribune, Manila
Bulletin, GMA, Philstar, PIA). A rule counts as **confirmed** when the regulation text was seen or two or more
independent sources agree. Anything else is **UNVERIFIED** here and shows "Unconfirmed: check with the agency" in
the app. Section 6 lists every such point for your confirmation.
