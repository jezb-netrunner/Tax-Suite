# JEZ Tax Suite: Audit of November 8, 2026

Branch `claude/laughing-babbage-90379l`, from `main` at 2186255. Audit date: **November 8, 2026** (Asia/Manila).
This follows the October 7, 2026 review (REVIEW.md, 78 findings, all fixed). It re-checks the rules against the law in
force today, hunts for bugs in the calculators, the calendar and the screens, and fixes what it found.

## 1. Summary for the owner

**Status: the rules and dates are current to November 8, 2026, with the gaps below fixed.** The core rate tables
(graduated income tax, 8%, OSD, percentage tax, VAT, corporate RCIT/MCIT, the four withholding tables, SSS,
PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, penalties) were re-checked and are right. Every generated deadline for 2026-2027 was checked
against the 2026 and 2027 holiday lists and the roll-over rules: no wrong dates were found.

What was wrong or missing, in order of importance:

1. **One calculator could halve the percentage tax (E1, Critical).** When sales passed ₱3M and the user picked an
   early month (e.g. March) but left the optional "sales January to March" box blank, the app spread the year's
   sales evenly and taxed only ₱1.5M of a ₱6M year: ₱45,000 instead of at least ₱90,000. Fixed.
2. **New invoice rule not in the app (A1, High).** Since **October 16, 2026** (RR 6-2026), an invoice for a sale the
   buyer withholds tax on must show the creditable withholding tax as a separate line. RMC 112-2026 (Oct 22, 2026)
   gives computerized systems until **Dec 31, 2026** and cash registers/POS until **Mar 31, 2027**. Added to the
   checklist, the calendar (for CAS and CRM/POS users) and the References.
3. **Metro Manila holidays on a tax deadline (A2, High).** Proclamation No. 1447 s.2026 made **Nov 16-18, 2026**
   special non-working days in NCR only (ASEAN Summit). The Q3 1701Q falls due **Mon Nov 16**. The calendar keeps the
   national date (always safe) and now says that Nov 16 is an NCR holiday and that no BIR advisory moving NCR
   deadlines was found.
4. **Two screens could crash or mislead after unusual events (S1, S2, High).** A link like `#/blog/50%off` blanked the
   whole app; after a failed save the Estimator showed old figures while the box showed the new ones. Both fixed; the
   app now has an error screen with a way back and a "download a copy of my data" button.
5. **Smaller law updates:** the SEC moved the 2025 AFS deadline to June 15, 2026 (A3); the Pag-IBIG employer schedule
   was replaced by HDMF Circular 499-2026 (A4, now marked unconfirmed); business closure follows RMC 47-2026 (A5); the
   micro-taxpayer abatement fee is ₱5,000 **per taxable year** (A6); a reminder for freelancers to give clients their
   sworn declaration so they withhold 5% instead of 10% (A7).

Nothing in this audit was deployed. Nothing was pushed to `main`. No live database was touched.

### Limits of the legal check (please read)

From this environment the BIR, SEC, PhilHealth, SSS, Pag-IBIG, Official Gazette and law-library websites could not be
opened (the network proxy blocks them). The law was checked by web search: search-indexed BIR documents, firm alerts
(Grant Thornton, KPMG, PwC, Forvis Mazars), and news reports (PIA, Philstar, BusinessWorld, Tribune, GMA, Inquirer).
A rule counts as confirmed when two or more independent sources agree; anything else is marked **UNVERIFIED** here
and "Unconfirmed: check with the agency" in the app.

## 4. Open questions for the CPA (UNVERIFIED)

1. **NCR deadlines on Nov 16-18, 2026.** Does the BIR (or SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG) move a deadline that falls on an
   NCR-only special non-working day to Nov 19? The app keeps Nov 16 (safe either way).
2. **Pag-IBIG due days under HDMF Circular 499-2026.** The app shows the 10th (the earliest old window) as unconfirmed.
3. **Sworn declaration deadline.** Is January 15 still the yearly deadline for the payee's sworn declaration?
4. **MCIT for fiscal-year corporations.** Is "the year of registration" in RR 9-98 the taxable year that contains the
   registration date (the app's reading)? If yes, should the profile ask for the registration month?
5. **NOLCO on the 1702Q.** May NOLCO be deducted on the quarterly corporate return? The quarter view leaves it out.
6. **Crossing ₱3M without monthly sales (E1).** Owner decision 6 said to spread the year's sales evenly; the app now
   uses ₱3,000,000 when that spread contradicts the month chosen. Confirm this floor (or require the actual sales).
7. **Digital services VAT.** RMC 47-2025 (per firm summaries) has every business buyer, VAT or non-VAT, remit 12% VAT
   on Form 1600-VT for digital services bought from nonresident providers (ads, software, cloud). Should the checklist
   remind businesses of this?
8. **Old tracking marks.** Profiles saved while "Mark as filed" existed still carry `filed` and `checklistDone`; nothing
   reads them. Strip them on the next save (data minimisation), or keep them as now?
